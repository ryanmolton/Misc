# Viasat: The Quest for Connection (8-bit)

An 88-second 8-bit history of Viasat, told by Mark Dankberg: from a spare
bedroom in Carlsbad (1986) to ViaSat-3 and the Moon.

**Watch:** `viasat_8bit_story.mp4` (1920×1080, 30 fps, stereo AAC)

Everything is generated by `make_video.py`: pixel art, sprites, animation,
the chiptune soundtrack, and every sound effect. The only external assets are two
open-license (OFL) pixel fonts in `fonts/`.

| World | Era | Graphics pipeline | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title | — | NES palette | NES fanfare |
| 1 · 1986 | Spare bedroom, Carlsbad | Game Boy: 160×90, 4 greens | 2 pulse channels + wave |
| 2 · Early years | DAMA modems, 1996 IPO | NES 54-colour palette | NES march, triangle bass, noise snare |
| 3 · 2011 | ViaSat-1 built and launched | SNES 15-bit colour | Pads, then a liftoff anthem |
| 4 · Everywhere | Homes, aviation, maritime, defense, Inmarsat | SNES | Bouncy montage |
| 5 · Today | ViaSat-3 constellation | HD colour, nebula, city lights | Brass and timpani, epic |
| ∞ · Next | Quantum-safe smallsats, cyber, autonomy, Moon | HD | Lydian FM bells |
| End | Back to the bedroom window | HD + a Game Boy window | Music-box callback, then finale |

The same leitmotif, the "Viasat theme", is re-orchestrated for every era.
Mark's sprite ages too (dark hair → salt-and-pepper → white) and changes
outfits: 80s sweater, shirt and tie, cleanroom bunny suit, pilot cap, captain's hat,
headset, blazer, astronaut helmet.

## Regenerate

```bash
pip install pillow numpy scipy imageio-ffmpeg
python3 make_video.py                 # full render (~5 min)
python3 make_video.py --preview 12 40 # PNG stills at given seconds
python3 make_video.py --audio-only    # rebuild + remux just the soundtrack
```

Script, timing, and dialog live in `SCENES` and `DIALOG` at the top of
`make_video.py`. Text boxes fit three lines of 31 characters.
